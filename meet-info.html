Meet Rules

REDLINE is built around cars, bikes, trucks and the people who love them. Keep the meet safe, respectful and enjoyable for everyone.

01 / EVERYONE

General Rules

  • Respect the property and surrounding businesses.
  • No fighting, harassment, threats or unnecessary drama.
  • Keep trash off the ground. Use designated trash cans.
  • Respect other people's vehicles, bikes and equipment.
  • Do not touch another person's vehicle without permission.
  • Follow instructions from REDLINE organizers and event staff.
  • Minors must follow all applicable laws and remain responsible for their own safety.
  • Illegal activity will not be tolerated.
02 / CARS

Cars

  • No burnouts, donuts or drifting in the meet area.
  • No excessive speeding through the parking lot.
  • Keep revving reasonable and respect nearby residents and businesses.
  • Do not block entrances, exits, emergency lanes or traffic flow.
  • Show cars only in designated areas.
  • Keep doors, hoods and trunks controlled when people are nearby.
  • Secure loose items and equipment around your vehicle.
  • Drive responsibly when entering and leaving the event.
03 / BIKES

Bikes

  • No wheelies, stoppies or stunt riding in the meet area.
  • No racing or high-speed riding around attendees.
  • Wear proper safety equipment while riding.
  • Keep motorcycles in designated parking/display areas.
  • Do not start or rev a bike directly beside spectators.
  • Respect other riders and never move another person's motorcycle.
  • Follow all Tennessee traffic laws when entering and leaving.
  • Ride responsibly and watch for pedestrians.
04 / TRUCKS

Trucks

  • No unnecessary revving or excessive exhaust noise.
  • No burnouts, donuts or aggressive driving.
  • Keep large vehicles clear of pedestrian areas.
  • Do not block entrances, emergency access or other vehicles.
  • Use caution when parking, backing up or leaving the meet.
  • Respect other builds regardless of style or modification.
  • Keep trailers and equipment in approved areas.
  • Leave enough room for emergency vehicles to pass.
05 / RESPECT

Noise

  • Keep music at a reasonable level.
  • No intentionally excessive exhaust noise.
  • No repeatedly revving engines to disturb others.
  • Respect nearby neighborhoods and businesses.
  • Organizers may ask vehicles to reduce noise.
06 / SAFETY

Safety First

  • Pedestrians always have priority.
  • Keep emergency lanes and access points clear.
  • Report dangerous behavior to event organizers.
  • Do not participate in reckless driving.
  • Emergency situations should be reported immediately.
  • REDLINE reserves the right to remove unsafe participants.

⚠ Event Policy

REDLINE is a community event. Participation is voluntary and everyone is responsible for their own actions, vehicles and property. REDLINE does not encourage street racing, reckless driving, illegal activity or unsafe vehicle operation. Follow all applicable laws and instructions from event organizers, property owners and law enforcement.

JULY 7, 2026

IT STARTED WITH AN IDEA.

REDLINE was founded on July 7th, 2026 by Itz_rymo, Alexa, and thtgsxrtho.

The idea was simple — create something that Dickson, Tennessee could call its own.

REDLINE was created to give the local car, motorcycle, and truck community a place to meet, connect, and enjoy the culture in a legal and respectful environment.

What started as a small idea quickly became something much bigger.

JULY 7, 2026

REDLINE IS BORN

REDLINE was officially founded by Itz_rymo, Alexa, and thtgsxrtho with the goal of creating a legal, respectful automotive and motorcycle meet for Dickson, Tennessee.

THE FIRST MEET

THE COMMUNITY SHOWED UP

What started with a few people turned into the first REDLINE meet — bringing together cars, motorcycles, trucks, and people from the local area.

JULY 15, 2026

REDLINE MERCH WAS DESIGNED

Just days after REDLINE was founded, the first basic REDLINE merch design was created. The goal was to bring the REDLINE name to the public and give the community something they could proudly represent.

AUGUST 21, 2026

THE SECOND MEET

REDLINE held its second meet, and it was bigger than the first. More riders, trucks, and cars showed up as word continued to spread and the community began to grow.

TODAY

REDLINE IS EXPANDING

REDLINE is now expanding across the state of Tennessee while staying rooted in its local community. New creators, riders, drivers, and members are joining from areas including Jackson, Nashville, and beyond.

REDLINE
CREATOR TEAM

REDLINE Creators are more than people who put a logo in their bio. We're looking for active creators who want to help build the community, show up for events, create content and help put REDLINE in front of more people.

Apply to Join How It Works
This is a partnership.

If you're accepted, you're representing REDLINE. We want creators who are willing to participate, promote the brand and actually help us grow.

01 / The Program

Not just another
creator list.

The REDLINE Creator Team is built around people who genuinely want to be involved. You don't have to be the biggest creator in Tennessee. What matters more is that you are active, reliable and willing to help REDLINE reach new people.

What we're looking for

Motorcycle, car and truck creators, photographers, videographers and automotive personalities who regularly post on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or other social platforms. We want people who can create quality content and naturally work REDLINE into what they already do.

Important

Activity matters.

This isn't a title you receive and forget about. Accepted creators are expected to stay active and contribute to the community.

02 / How It Works

Four steps.
No guessing.

We want the expectations to be clear before you apply.

01 / APPLY

Tell us about yourself

Send your social links, what you create, what you drive or ride, and why you think REDLINE is a good fit.

02 / SUPPORT

Own REDLINE first

Before final acceptance, creators must purchase at least one REDLINE clothing item or decal. This helps show that you genuinely support the brand you're asking to represent.

03 / REVIEW

We review your application

We look at your content, activity, attitude and whether you are someone who can positively contribute to REDLINE.

04 / JOIN

Start creating

Approved creators receive their REDLINE creator information and a creator code they can share with their audience.

03 / Your Partnership

What you
actually get.

We're keeping the program realistic. REDLINE is growing, and we would rather offer a few useful benefits than promise things we can't provide.

01 / CREATOR CODE

A code to share

You'll receive a REDLINE creator code that your followers can use for an available discount on qualifying REDLINE merch. There is no commission or shop-credit system tied to the code.

02 / EXPOSURE

Be part of REDLINE

Approved creators may be featured on REDLINE social media, the website, event content and other community posts when the content is a good fit.

03 / ACCESS

Build with us

Creators can help with event coverage, meet promotion, community content and future REDLINE projects as the program grows.

Before Final Acceptance

Support the brand first.

REDLINE isn't giving away free creator merch right now. Before an applicant can be officially accepted as a Creator / Partner, they must purchase at least one REDLINE clothing item or decal. This isn't about spending a certain amount — it's about showing that you are willing to support the brand you want to represent.

04 / Expectations

We need
active partners.

Being a REDLINE Creator means helping the business and community, not simply putting a title next to your username.

Stay active

Continue creating and posting. We aren't looking for accounts that only become active when there is something free to receive.

Promote REDLINE

Help people discover REDLINE events, the website, social pages, merch and the community when it naturally fits your content.

Use your creator code

Share your code when promoting REDLINE merch or posting content where your audience may be interested. The code is for your audience's discount.

Represent the community

Treat riders, drivers, creators, businesses and event locations with respect. Your public actions can reflect on REDLINE.

Keep it legal

REDLINE does not promote street racing, reckless driving or illegal activity. Content should not encourage behavior that puts people or the community at risk.

Help us grow

Introduce new people to the community, bring energy to events and help make REDLINE something people want to be part of.
